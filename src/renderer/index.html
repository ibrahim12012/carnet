            Fallback mode

            Use your phone as a camera

            1. Put the phone on the same Wi-Fi as the computer. If Windows asks whether to allow Carnet on the network, accept.
            2. Put the phone on the same Wi-Fi as the Mac. If macOS asks whether Carnet may accept incoming connections or find devices on your local network, allow it.
            3. Scan this QR code with the phone’s camera.
            4. The first time, the browser warns that the connection is “not private”: tap Show Details (iPhone) or Advanced (Android), then visit this website or Proceed. This is normal: the page comes from your computer, not from the Internet.
            5. Tap Start camera. Then choose below who records: the phone (picture and sound at full quality) or this computer.

            The video does not get through between the phone and the computer.

            The phone does not reach the computer?
            • Check that both are on the same Wi-Fi (not the phone’s 4G/5G, not a “guest” network).
            • The Windows firewall may be blocking Carnet: (Windows asks for confirmation)
            • macOS may be blocking Carnet: in System Settings, turn on Carnet in Privacy & Security > Local Network, and if the firewall is on, allow incoming connections for Carnet in Network > Firewall > Options.
            • If the page opens on the phone but stays stuck, close the tab and scan the QR code again.

            Create a free key at aistudio.google.com/apikey and paste it here. With transcription by Gemini, the sound of each entry (not the picture) is sent to Google; with whisper, only the text.             Search memory

            When your journal is too big to be sent whole with each video, Carnet sends your most recent subjects in full, finds the older ones close to what the video says (by meaning and by name), and sends the others by their title. The search uses vectors made by Gemini (a separate quota: your calls of the day do not change), kept in search-memory.json in the journal folder.

            Permanent facts

            Recording

            The phone records the picture and the sound itself, at its full quality, and sends the file to the computer as it goes: the speed of this computer does not matter. Light quality is for a computer that cannot compress the video fast enough (the preview is smooth but the recording jerky); Carnet turns it on by itself when a recording loses too many frames. Both choices are also in the recording window.

            Daily reminder

            A notification with what there is to check: a realization or something you left behind whose time has come, a look-back, a subject that stopped coming up. When there is nothing to check, where you stood a month ago. Clicking it opens Carnet.

            While the reminder is on, Carnet starts with Windows without opening its window, and stays in the notification area, next to the clock, when you close it. To quit it, right-click its icon there.

            While the reminder is on, Carnet opens by itself when you log in, without its window, and its icon stays in the menu bar. To quit it, click that icon, then Quit Carnet. The first time, macOS asks whether Carnet may send notifications: allow it.

            Language

            The language of Carnet and of what Gemini writes. Transcripts stay in the language you speak.

            Export all data

            Everything Carnet knows about your journal, in a single file: readable text, raw data, or an archive with the videos.
